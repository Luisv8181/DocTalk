# DocTalk

**The open resource center for the mental health of medical students, residents, and physicians.**

DocTalk helps people find, evaluate, and use credible mental-health and wellbeing resources without having to search the medical ecosystem one institution at a time.

## Why DocTalk exists

The research foundation points to a paradox: there are many resources, but they are fragmented, difficult to compare, inconsistently documented, and often poorly aligned to audience and access constraints.

DocTalk is therefore not another wellness program. It is a **discovery and curation layer**.

## The knowledge model

Every resource should be understandable through a consistent set of questions:

**Who is it for? What problem does it address? What kind of resource is it? Where can I access it? What does it cost? What evidence supports it? What are the privacy/confidentiality implications? What are its limitations? When was it last reviewed?**

The canonical structured dataset lives in `data/resources.yml`.

## Safety

DocTalk is an informational resource navigator, not a diagnostic, treatment, or crisis-triage service.

Emergency resources must remain prominent and current. The project should not require sensitive personal information. See [Safety](docs/safety.md).

## Build philosophy

**Repository first. Interface second. Intelligence last.**

Build the trustworthy dataset and curation system before adding sophisticated AI. The future navigator should retrieve from approved resources and show provenance rather than generating unsupported recommendations.
