DOCTALK · NEEDS WALL

What would help the people who practice medicine?

A public record of unmet needs and tool ideas shared by medical students, residents, and physicians — the demand signal behind the catalog. Listing a need is not an endorsement of any solution, and nothing here is clinical advice.

In immediate danger or crisis? Use an appropriate emergency or crisis service. The Needs Wall is not for requesting clinical advice, diagnosis, or crisis help — those requests are redirected, not discussed. DocTalk is a resource navigator, not a crisis-triage or treatment service.

The wall.

Each need is transcribed from a real submission — a community discussion, a form, or published survey findings with recorded provenance. Maintainers never invent needs. Share yours in the Needs & ideas discussion.

DocTalk is an informational discovery project. Inclusion does not equal endorsement.
