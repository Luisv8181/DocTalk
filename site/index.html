DOCTALK

Mental health resources for the people who practice medicine.

A curated, open discovery layer for medical students, residents, physicians, educators, and mental-health professionals.

In immediate danger or crisis? Use an appropriate emergency or crisis service. DocTalk is a resource navigator, not a crisis-triage or treatment service.

Find the right door.

Start with who you are and what you need. DocTalk is designed to make a fragmented resource landscape easier to navigate.

DocTalk is an informational discovery project. Inclusion does not equal endorsement.
