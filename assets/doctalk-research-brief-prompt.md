# DocTalk Visual Brief

The visual research brief should communicate, at a glance:
- the mental-health burden in medical education/practice
- contributing factors
- barriers to help-seeking
- existing resource categories
- fragmentation/gaps
- the DocTalk opportunity
- the phased build roadmap

Use the latest generated DocTalk research infographic as the visual reference. Prefer clean medical editorial design, high readability, strong hierarchy, restrained color, and accessible contrast. Avoid decorative human imagery that adds no explanatory value.
