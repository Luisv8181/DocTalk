# DocTalk Evidence Framework

DocTalk should make the evidence behind resources visible without pretending that every resource can be reduced to a single score.

## Suggested labels

### Research-supported
There is relevant peer-reviewed empirical evidence supporting the intervention or resource.

### Evidence-informed
The resource is grounded in established evidence or professional standards, but direct outcome evidence may be limited.

### Professional/expert guidance
Produced by a credible professional or expert organization without a strong direct intervention evidence base.

### Institutional resource
A legitimate service, program, policy, or support mechanism operated by an institution.

### Peer/community resource
A peer-support or community resource where lived experience and community utility are important, but formal intervention evidence may be limited.

### Informational
Primarily educational or informational material.

## Important distinction

A low evidence label does not automatically mean a resource is harmful or useless. Conversely, a resource from a prestigious organization should not automatically be described as evidence-based.

DocTalk should preserve the underlying evidence and explain limitations where possible.
