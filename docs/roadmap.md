- [x] Create repository

## Phase 1 — MVP resource center
Target: ~50 carefully verified high-value resources across major categories.

Categories:
1. crisis and immediate support
2. peer support
3. counseling/EAP
4. physician health
5. burnout/stress/professional fulfillment
6. depression/anxiety/suicide prevention
7. trauma/grief/difficult clinical experiences
8. education/curricula
9. assessment/research
10. equity/culture/mentorship/belonging
11. AI/technology/privacy

Deliverables:
- canonical resource dataset
- evidence taxonomy
- source/provenance pages
- searchable static site
- accessibility baseline
- review-date tracking

## Phase 2 — Discovery
- audience filters
- problem/topic filters
- geography
- cost/access
- privacy/confidentiality
- evidence
- time/format
- language
- emergency support prominence
- resource comparison

## Phase 3 — Community
- pull-request contribution model
- resource request issues
- stale-resource reporting
- specialty maintainers
- student/resident/physician reviewers
- partnerships with professional and educational organizations

## Phase 4 — Intelligent navigation
- natural-language search over canonical metadata
- explanation of why a resource matches
- citations/provenance on every recommendation
- confidence and limitations
- no diagnosis
- no autonomous emergency triage

## Phase 5 — Research and impact
Measure:
- resource coverage
- freshness
- geographic coverage
- evidence coverage
- search-to-resource click-through
- contributor growth
- user-reported usefulness
- institutional adoption
- citations/educational use

## Build order
Do not start with an elaborate AI chatbot. Build the dataset and trustworthy retrieval layer first. The quality of the future navigator will be limited by the quality of the underlying catalog.
