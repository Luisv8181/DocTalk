# DocTalk Verification Checklist

Before promoting a resource to `verified-entry`, check:

1. Official URL resolves.
2. Organization identity is clear.
3. Audience/eligibility is accurate.
4. Access method is accurate.
5. Cost is current or explicitly marked unknown/variable.
6. Region/jurisdiction is accurate.
7. Hours are only listed when currently documented.
8. Confidentiality/privacy claims are directly supported.
9. Clinical-use claims are not overstated.
10. Emergency designation is appropriate.
11. Language access is documented or marked unknown.
12. Evidence classification reflects the actual resource, not the reputation of its sponsor.
13. Any licensing/permission restrictions are noted.
14. Last-reviewed date is updated.
15. Limitations are stated where material.

For emergency or crisis services, verify the access pathway with particular care and prioritize official sources.
