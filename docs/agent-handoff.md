## Current source of truth
The repository's structured catalog in `data/resources.yml` is canonical for resource metadata.

## Current work
The project is transitioning from foundation into catalog expansion and web-interface construction.

## What to do next
Choose one of the open GitHub issues and implement the smallest complete increment. Preserve the canonical schema and safety boundaries.

## What not to do
- Do not invent resources.
- Do not scrape or reproduce copyrighted content wholesale.
- Do not build an AI therapist.
- Do not add hidden analytics or personal-data collection.
- Do not merge unverified crisis/contact details.
- Do not treat one organization's existence as evidence that every intervention it offers is effective.

## Handoff expectation
Every meaningful change should leave the repository easier for the next agent to understand.
